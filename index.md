# Optimum for operators

A gateway runs beside your beacon node, joins the Optimum mesh, and is what delivers blocks to that node. [Console](https://console.getoptimum.io/) is where you enrol it, connect your validators, and read the result.

[Open Console](https://console.getoptimum.io/)

New to this: [What Optimum does](/start/what-optimum-does).

The gateway is already running and you need a setting, a metric, or a failure: [Gateway reference](https://getoptimum.github.io/optimum-gateway/versions/latest/).
