# Account type

The question is **How do you take part?**

| | Individual | Entity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who | You take part under your own name. | A company, fund, or staking provider. |
| Next screen | **Your details**. Contact fields. | **Your organization**. Entity fields, then the same contact fields. |
| What you can use | Signal. | Signal, and Accelerate where Optimum enables it. |

Pick **Entity** if you want Accelerate. Optimum enables it per organization, and the sidebar also needs at least 200 active validators on mainnet. Picking Entity does not enable it on its own. See [What Accelerate does](/accelerate/what-accelerate-does).

The stored account type for an individual is an internal id. The label on the screen is **Individual**.

Running validators is the next question, on the registration screen, not this one. Answering no is a supported account. It selects the stream-only gateway. It does not block registration.
